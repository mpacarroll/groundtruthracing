Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-24. Updated 2026-11-26 to add the Race Weekend Brief email list.

Ground Truth Racing runs a private publishing app that uploads videos it made to the social media accounts it owns. This policy covers that app, this website and the free Race Weekend Brief email list.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (to confirm which account is connected) and permission to upload videos to that account's drafts.
  • X: permission to upload media and publish posts on the Ground Truth Racing account.
  • Instagram and YouTube: permission to publish videos and to read the account's own recent posts, so the same video is never posted twice.

The only accounts connected are Ground Truth Racing's own. The app does not access, collect, or store anyone else's data, and it does not read followers, messages, or other users' content.

What is stored

Access tokens are stored as secrets in the app's private hosting environment, never in source code. The app keeps a record of which videos it has sent, by platform post id. Nothing else is stored.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared. Videos go only to the platform they are posted on.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The host may log visitor IP addresses for security, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The Race Weekend Brief email list

If you sign up on the brief page:

  • We collect your email address, and nothing else.
  • We use it to send the free Race Weekend Brief before each race weekend, and nothing else.
  • Our email provider, Kit, stores the list for us. We do not sell, rent or share it.
  • The sign-up form sends your address straight to Kit. This site adds no tracking pixels, no ad scripts and no analytics for it.
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted, reply to any email or send a direct message to @groundtruthracing on Instagram.
  • Every email carries the business mailing address below.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's connected-apps settings, after which the app can do nothing on that account.
